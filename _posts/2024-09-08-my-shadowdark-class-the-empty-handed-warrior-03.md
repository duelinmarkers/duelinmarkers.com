I've just put something on Itch.io for the first time! It's a PC class for ShadowDark RPG called [The Empty-Handed Warrior](https://duelinmarkers.itch.io/empty-handed-warrior-sd). It's free to download, and I'd love playtest feedback.

I've been thinking for a while about an unarmed fighter for Shadowdark RPG. (Actually I put up [some earlier drafts of this idea on SD Discord](https://discord.com/channels/558029475837902851/1148371556473516073) about a year ago.) This is inspired in part by 5e's Monk but is intended to cover other weaponless fighter archetypes as well.

Some goals:
- Provide interesting tactical choices without bogging down combat—Shadowdark's quick turns are precious!
- Don't be better at fighting than the Fighter class.
- Don't declare any ability "special" that every PC should have, effectively stealing freedom from other players and the GM.
